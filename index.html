A guided pathway for deciding who needs post-operative troponin surveillance and how to interpret an elevated post-op troponin.

Teaching frame: do not start with the troponin number alone. Start with the clinical question, operation/stress, patient reserve, RCRI, BNP/NT-proBNP, then interpret troponin with ECG, symptoms, trajectory, and competing oxygen supply-demand triggers.
