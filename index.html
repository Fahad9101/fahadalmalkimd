Clinical Tools

Practical decision-support tools

A collection of tools focused on antithrombotic therapy, thrombosis care, inpatient risk assessment, and shared decision-making. These tools support structured clinical thinking and patient-centered discussions; they do not replace clinical judgment, local policy, or specialist consultation.

AF Anticoagulation Shared Decision Aid

Structured support for anticoagulation discussions in atrial fibrillation, including estimated stroke risk, bleeding risk, anticoagulant choice, and situations where warfarin remains preferred, such as moderate-to-severe mitral stenosis or rheumatic valvular heart disease.

CRFT

Clinical Reasoning Framework for Teaching

CRFT is an educational project designed to help residents practice, structure, and improve clinical reasoning through cases, domain-based scoring, feedback, and reflection.

Core domains

Problem framing Hypothesis generation Data interpretation Management planning Anticipation Metacognition

Purpose

To move clinical reasoning teaching from informal impressions to a structured, observable, and teachable process. Residents frame problems clearly, generate prioritized differentials, interpret data in context, identify dangerous diagnoses, and reflect on how thinking evolves.

Current status

CRFT is being developed as a teaching and quality-improvement tool for resident clinical reasoning education.

QI Projects

Quality improvement and systems design

Projects focused on inpatient internal medicine, care transitions, thrombosis prevention, perioperative medicine, and diagnostic safety.

CTU Early Readmission & Transitional Care Optimization

Identifying preventable patterns in patients readmitted within seven days, including diagnostic uncertainty, medication issues, pending investigations, follow-up gaps, social barriers, and discharge readiness concerns.

VTE Prophylaxis Standardization

Improving VTE prophylaxis processes for medical inpatients by clarifying prophylaxis status, reducing documentation ambiguity, improving dashboard reliability, and standardizing decision-making.

Perioperative MINS Screening

Supporting postoperative troponin screening, risk recognition, and follow-up pathways for high-risk surgical patients after non-cardiac surgery.

Internal Medicine Diagnostic Service Scope

Clarifying the role of General Internal Medicine as a diagnostic and admitting service for complex, undifferentiated, or multisystem presentations.
