          F. Guideline anchors

          • Acute VTE generally requires a minimum treatment phase when anticoagulation is not contraindicated.
          • For AF, DOACs are generally preferred over warfarin except mechanical valves or moderate-to-severe mitral stenosis; aspirin is not a substitute for anticoagulation when OAC is indicated.
          • Heparin/LMWH and fondaparinux activity depends on antithrombin biology; unexpectedly low anti-Xa or heparin resistance physiology should prompt review of dose, timing, assay calibration, renal function, AT activity, and clinical context.
          • PF4-mediated thrombosis syndromes include classic HIT, delayed/autoimmune HIT, spontaneous HIT-like syndromes, and VITT; syndrome pattern and exposure history guide testing and treatment.
          • INVICTUS supports vitamin K antagonist therapy over rivaroxaban in rheumatic heart disease-associated atrial fibrillation.
          • Interpret all recommendations against local policy, bleeding risk, patient goals, and specialist input.
